### Key Findings

* Overall view-to-purchase conversion decreased from **7.09% in January to 6.79% in February**, a **0.30 percentage point decline**.
* `apparel.glove` was the strongest category with a **38.91% view-to-purchase conversion rate**.
* `accessories.bag` had weak funnel performance with **4.64% view-to-cart** and **1.43% view-to-purchase conversion**.
* `runail` had the highest purchase volume with **45,274 purchases**, while `bpw.style` had a stronger conversion rate of **22.95%**.
* Approximately **6.91% of purchase prices** were identified as outliers using the IQR method, with an upper threshold of approximately **11.515**.

### Conclusion

The main opportunities are improving low-performing categories, optimizing high-view/low-conversion products, investigating the February conversion decline, and reviewing unusual price values.
